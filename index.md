# Rewind Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 12, 2026_

Rewind ("the app") is an Android app that keeps a searchable history of the notifications your
phone receives. This policy explains what it does with that information. The short version:
**everything stays on your device.** Rewind has no account, no server and no network access.

## What Rewind accesses

Rewind uses Android's Notification access, which you grant yourself in system settings. With it,
the app receives each notification your phone posts, including:

- the app that sent it, the time it arrived, its title and its text (including expanded text,
  and message content from messaging apps);
- how it left the notification shade (for example dismissed, opened, or removed by the app).

Notifications can contain personal or sensitive content, such as messages and one-time codes.
Rewind treats all of it as private.

## Where it goes

Notifications are saved in a database **on your device only**. Rewind does not:

- send notification content, or anything else, to the developer or to any third party;
- use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking libraries;
- request the internet permission, so the app cannot send data off your device.

The developer cannot see your notifications.

## What stays on your device, and how you control it

- **Excluded apps:** in Settings you can exclude any app so its notifications are never saved.
- **Revoke access:** turn off Rewind's Notification access in Android settings at any time. Rewind
  then stops saving new notifications.
- **Delete your data:** uninstall Rewind, or use Android Settings > Apps > Rewind > Storage >
  Clear data. This permanently removes everything the app has saved.
- **Backups:** Rewind opts out of Android's cloud backup, so its history is not copied to your
  Google account or restored on a new phone.

## Clipboard

When you tap "Copy code" on a saved notification, Rewind places that code on your clipboard
(flagged as sensitive where Android supports it). Rewind does not read your clipboard.

## Children

Rewind is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
